# Release notes

User-facing changes to Arc TV for Mac, newest first. This file is the release notes shown in the app's own update pop-up: whatever sits under a
version heading becomes that release's description. Add each change as a bullet under `## Unreleased` as you make it; publishing a release files
them under its version automatically.

## Unreleased

- Arc TV on your Mac: sign in with your account and browse Popular, New and Top rated movies and shows.
- Open any title, see its details and episodes, pick a source and watch it.
- Your sign-in is kept in a private file, so Arc TV never asks for your Mac password.
- A built-in player that plays 4K, HDR and Dolby Vision files, with audio and subtitle choices.
- Pick up where you left off: Continue Watching on Home remembers every title, on every device.
- Add titles to My List, mark them watched, or take them out of Continue Watching with a right-click.
- Manage your addons from the app: add one from a link, switch it off, or remove it.
- Hide the genres you never want to see, on every device.
- Like or mark Not for me on any title, and your Home gets a Picked for you row of titles chosen just for you (ArcTV Plus).
- See what shapes your picks in Settings, and start fresh any time.
- Right-click any poster for the web app's quick menu: Play, My List, Watched, Like and Not for me, all in one place.
- Home now shows the same rows as the web and Fire TV apps, with a rotating banner of featured titles.
- Watched titles get a green tick, and My List can show just what you have watched, in the order you like.
- Title pages now match the web app: logo, release date, trailer, IMDb rating, cast photos and more like this.
- Settings sits in the top bar with Home, Movies, TV Shows, Search and My List.
- Movies and TV Shows now have the web app's genre drop-down, search that suggests titles as you type, and recent searches.
- Home appears instantly with your last rows while the new ones load, and the top bar slides away as you scroll.
- Play carries on with the source you used before, and the player remembers your volume and speed.
- No sources found? A step-by-step guide now walks you through setting them up.
- A new sign-in page, with Sign Up built in, matches the web app.
- Profiles for everyone at home (ArcTV Plus): each gets their own My List, Continue Watching and picks, with kids profiles and optional PINs.
