# Release notes

User-facing changes to Arc TV for Mac, newest first. This file is the release notes shown in the app's own update pop-up: whatever sits under a
version heading becomes that release's description. Add each change as a bullet under `## Unreleased` as you make it; publishing a release files
them under its version automatically.

## Unreleased

- Arc TV on your Mac: sign in with your account and browse Popular, New and Top rated movies and shows.
- Open any title, see its details and episodes, pick a source and watch it.
- Your sign-in is kept in a private file, so Arc TV never asks for your Mac password.
- A built-in player that plays 4K, HDR and Dolby Vision files, with audio and subtitle choices.
- Pick up where you left off: Continue Watching on Home remembers every title, on every device.
- Add titles to My List, mark them watched, or take them out of Continue Watching with a right-click.
