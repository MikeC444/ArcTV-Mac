# Release notes

User-facing changes to Arc TV for Mac, newest first. This file is the release notes shown in the app's own update pop-up: whatever sits under a
version heading becomes that release's description. Add each change as a bullet under `## Unreleased

- Fixed a player issue.

## Unreleased

- Sources still being prepared now say "not ready yet" instead of playing a short clip.

## 0.1.0

- Arc TV on your Mac: sign in with your account and browse Popular, New and Top rated movies and shows.
- Open any title, see its details and episodes, pick a source and watch it.
- Your sign-in is kept in a private file, so Arc TV never asks for your Mac password.
- A built-in player that plays 4K, HDR and Dolby Vision files, with audio and subtitle choices.
- A new player layout like the Fire TV app, with a full-screen button, and a loading spinner in the middle whenever the video is loading or you jump to a new spot.
- Smoother playback: the player does far less work per frame, so it should feel lighter and less laggy.
- The three dots on a title's page now burst open with a springy, one-after-another pop, and the Who's watching screen shows the Arc TV logo.
- The player's Audio, Subtitles, Speed, Episodes and Player menus are now clean dark cards with a tick on what is chosen.
- Next episode: a button in the last minute, and the next episode starts by itself when one ends (switch it off in Settings > Player).
- Choose how big the torrent buffer is and how much disk a torrent may use, in Settings > Player.
- Arc TV now has its own app icon.
- Grey placeholders now hold the place of whatever is still loading (sources, cast, episodes, stats and more), and the player shows your movie's picture and logo while it starts.
- Pick up where you left off: Continue Watching on Home remembers every title, on every device.
- Add titles to My List, mark them watched, or take them out of Continue Watching with a right-click.
- Manage your addons from the app: add one from a link, switch it off, or remove it.
- Hide the genres you never want to see, on every device.
- Like or mark Not for me on any title, and your Home gets a Picked for you row of titles chosen just for you (ArcTV Plus).
- See what shapes your picks in Settings, and start fresh any time.
- Right-click any poster for the web app's quick menu: Play, My List, Watched, Like and Not for me, all in one place.
- Home now shows the same rows as the web and Fire TV apps, with a rotating banner of featured titles.
- Watched titles get a green tick, and My List can show just what you have watched, in the order you like.
- Title pages now match the web app: logo, release date, trailer, IMDb rating, cast photos and more like this.
- Settings sits in the top bar with Home, Movies, TV Shows, Search and My List.
- Movies and TV Shows now have the web app's genre drop-down, search that suggests titles as you type, and recent searches.
- Home appears instantly with your last rows while the new ones load, and the top bar slides away as you scroll.
- Play carries on with the source you used before, and the player remembers your volume and speed.
- No sources found? A step-by-step guide now walks you through setting them up.
- A new sign-in page, with Sign Up built in, matches the web app.
- Profiles for everyone at home (ArcTV Plus): each gets their own My List, Continue Watching and picks, with kids profiles and optional PINs.
- The Arc TV Plus tab shows the plans and opens secure checkout in your browser, and lets you cancel any time.
- Your stats at a glance: total watch time, streak, a 13-week activity map and your busiest day.
- Smart source picking starts the best source for you, and a short Plus welcome tells members what they get.
- Arrange Home your way: switch rows on or off and move them up or down, saved to your account.
- Pick your subtitle and audio languages once and every video starts the way you like it.
- Check for updates right inside Settings and see what's new.
- Torrent sources now play right inside Arc TV, with your movie's artwork and logo on screen while it gets ready.
- Select a source looks like the web app: movie details on the left, quality filters and sorting, and richer source cards.
- A movie's page now matches the web app: artwork behind everything, cast at the bottom, and a bigger You May Also Like row under it.
- New versions are announced when you open Arc TV, with what's new and a Download button.
- Sign in by scanning a QR code with your phone.
- Move through search suggestions with the arrow keys, and press Return to open one.
- Account now shows which profile you are watching as.
- Arc TV Plus: save movies and episodes to your Mac with the new Download button, best quality at the smallest size first.
- Arc TV now needs a Mac with Apple Silicon and macOS 14 or later.
